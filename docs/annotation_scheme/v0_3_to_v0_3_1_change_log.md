# Annotation prompt v0.3 → v0.3.1 change log

v0.3.1 is a prompt-only calibration experiment. It uses the unchanged v0.3
JSON schema and validator, retains independent T/P/E/O scores, and does not
alter the high burden for O. The original v0.3 prompt remains versioned and
unchanged.

The visible E label changes from “exclamatory/reflexive” to the narrower
“expressive/reflexive”; the schema field remains `exclamatory_reflexive` for
compatibility. The revised prompt states that passion, exclamatory punctuation,
prosody, spontaneity, repetition, bodily arousal, and sexual context do not by
themselves support E. E now requires positive evidence that producing the words
functions as expressive or reflex-like discharge rather than merely as an
emotionally intense T avowal or P undertaking.

The prompt adds a counterfactual diagnostic: if T/P scores plus qualitative
context about intensity and delivery lose nothing important about the core
force, E should be zero. It continues to permit independently motivated T/E or
P/E mixtures and explicitly assigns genuine expressive/reflexive cases to E,
not O.

Synthetic contrasts calibrate passionate T, passionate P, reflective but
shouted T, and a candidate involuntary blurt. They are prompt aids rather than
literary corpus items or expected labels imposed on real cases.

The empirical purpose is not to force E downward. Comparing v0.3 with v0.3.1
tests whether high E scores survive with independent core-force explanations,
or fall when emotional delivery is represented as context instead.
