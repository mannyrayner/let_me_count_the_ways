# *Persuasion* as a recall-gap example

Extraction v0.8 produced zero candidates for *Persuasion*. The AI collaborator
recognized this as suspicious because it knew that the novel contains `I have
loved none but you` Inspection showed that the English perfect pattern required
`you` immediately after `loved` and did not allow the exclusive `none but`
complement.

This specific miss suggested a cross-linguistic class of exclusive-target LOVE
constructions. The systematic deterministic audit therefore considered, among
other bounded forms, French `Je n'aime que toi` and Swedish `Jag älskar ingen
annan än dig`, rather than applying a one-off English literal patch.

> The useful contribution arose from combining literary knowledge, linguistic generalization and awareness of the computational extraction mechanism.
