# Indie-romance pilot rights-review exclusions

Reviewed on 2026-09-05 from the purchased ebooks in Thorium Reader.

- *The Corners Of My Heart* by Khulasande Dumisa was excluded after inspection
  of in-book rights information.
- *Error of Understanding* by Stella McMillan was excluded after inspection of
  in-book rights information. This exclusion was superseded on 2026-09-07 by
  explicit author permission for the required academic-research text; see
  `mcmillan-error-of-understanding-author-permission-2026-09-07.md`.

The full purchased files and any derived full text are not corpus artifacts and
must not be committed. The later permission allows required research excerpts
from *Error of Understanding*, but not its full ebook or full converted text.

This review demonstrated that external storefront metadata alone was
insufficient: inspection of the actual ebooks revealed contradictory rights
information. The note intentionally does not reproduce unnecessary copyright
text from either book.
