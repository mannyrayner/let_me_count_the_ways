# Research Plan

## Research question

How do the semantics and pragmatic functions of “I love you” vary across textual
contexts, genres, speaker relationships, and historical or cultural settings?

## Initial phases

1. Define corpus eligibility, licensing, provenance, and sampling criteria.
2. Assemble a small development corpus for method design.
3. Develop an ontology and annotation scheme using human review and model-assisted
   exploration.
4. Evaluate annotation reliability and model validity.
5. Acquire and process a larger, diverse corpus.
6. Run quantitative analyses and robustness checks.
7. Document limitations and prepare reproducible research outputs.

## Open design decisions

- Unit of context surrounding each occurrence
- Corpus sources and sampling strata
- Semantic and pragmatic categories
- Treatment of quotation, irony, negation, translation, and reported speech
- Human-validation sample size and reliability measures
- OpenAI model selection, prompt design, and structured output schema
- Privacy, bias, licensing, and reproducibility requirements

## Reproducibility requirements

Record source identifiers, licenses, retrieval dates, content hashes, processing
versions, prompt versions, model snapshots, API parameters, random seeds where
applicable, and run-level costs and error logs.
