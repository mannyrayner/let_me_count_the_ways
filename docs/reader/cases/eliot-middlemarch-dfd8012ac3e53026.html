Ambiguity noted by the annotator

The surrounding exchange may suggest that Mary is withholding or indirectly concealing genuine love, but this pragmatic possibility does not turn the conditional clause itself into a direct avowal.

Narrative situation

The original annotator’s interpretation of the supplied context.

Fred presses Mary to say that she loves him and to promise marriage. Mary instead separates the two issues: even granting love hypothetically, she considers marriage to an idle, indebted man morally wrong. Her wording may strategically avoid disclosing her feelings, but its immediate force is conditional argument rather than confession, commitment, or reflexive expression.
