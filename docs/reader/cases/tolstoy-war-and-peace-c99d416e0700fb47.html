Princess Marya directly avows that she loves Mlle Bourienne “more than ever.” The phrase principally presents her continuing and even increased love as true. Its reassuring and forgiving use does not itself constitute a relational undertaking; the explicit commitment occurs in the following clause about doing everything in her power for Bourienne’s happiness.
