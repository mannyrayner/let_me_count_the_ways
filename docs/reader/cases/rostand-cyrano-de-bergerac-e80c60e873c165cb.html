His bare declaration is therefore framed as his own sincere avowal, but Roxane finds it rhetorically inadequate and repeatedly asks him to elaborate. Her dissatisfaction concerns eloquence, not the intelligibility of his assertion of love. Although the scene is courtship and Christian hopes for reciprocation, this particular utterance does not itself establish a commitment or undertaking.
