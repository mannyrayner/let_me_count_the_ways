Believe me,” I continued, seeing him make an impatient movement, “you exaggerate the consequences of this liaison. Marguerite is not the sort of girl you believe her to be. I was stronger in the face of his threats than in the face of his entreaties. “Who told you that I was going to hand that sum over to her?” I replied. “My notary. Would an honest man have drawn up such a deed without warning me? Well, I have come to Paris to prevent you from ruining yourself for the sake of a girl.
