How the scores were assigned

The highlighted words occur within Loredana’s categorical negative declaration, “io non vi amerò mai” (“I shall never love you”). Its core force is truth-conditional: she presents her present disposition and future emotional relation to Berto as one in which love is impossible. Although the declaration decisively rejects him, it does not substantially undertake a commitment in the narrow P sense. Its vehemence is fully representable as contextual intensity attached to T and supplies no independent evidence of reflex-like expressive discharge.

Narrative situation

The original annotator’s interpretation of the supplied context.

Loredana uses the declaration to dispel Berto’s hopes and explain that her contemplated relationship with him would be a self-destructive sacrifice for Filippo, whom she says she truly loves. Her contempt, hatred, and savage vehemence intensify the rejection but do not change its core truth-conditional force. The narration subsequently contrasts her denial with Berto’s sincere avowal of love.
