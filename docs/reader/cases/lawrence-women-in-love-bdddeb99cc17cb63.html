Narrative situation

The original annotator’s interpretation of the supplied context.

The passage contrasts ordinary verbal avowal with a mystical conception of union in which the separate “I” and “you” have disappeared. “I love you” is therefore presented as propositionally sincere but conceptually inadequate—speech belonging to separable persons—rather than as false, performative, or reflexive. The claim that their experience is “beyond love” concerns the limits of the formula and the surrounding metaphysics, not an unrepresented force within the formula itself.
