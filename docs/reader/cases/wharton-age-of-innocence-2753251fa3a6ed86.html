How the scores were assigned

Archer’s “At least I loved you” is principally a retrospective avowal presented as true: whatever their misunderstandings and losses, his love for Ellen was real. It does not itself undertake a future commitment. His difficulty in bringing out the words conveys emotional strain, but there is no independent evidence that they function as a reflex-like expressive discharge rather than a deeply felt assertion.
