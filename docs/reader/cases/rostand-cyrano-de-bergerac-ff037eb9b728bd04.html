French “je vous aime” can suggest romantic love, but Roxane’s references to tender friendship and her declared love for Christian strongly favor affectionate, nonromantic fondness here. The degree of expressive/reflexive force is less certain than the strong T force.

Narrative situation

The original annotator’s interpretation of the supplied context.

Roxane has just obtained Cyrano’s sworn promise to protect Christian, whom she romantically loves. Her line expresses warm gratitude and established tender friendship toward Cyrano, not a romantic declaration or promise of commitment. Its light, immediate placement and accompanying kiss support some expressive effusion; Cyrano’s subdued replies and subsequent immobility create dramatic irony because he loves her romantically, but that irony does not alter her utterance’s core force.
