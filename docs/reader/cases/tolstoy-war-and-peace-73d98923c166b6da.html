The original annotator’s interpretation of the supplied context.

The declaration softens a compassionate but definite rejection. Natasha has just told her mother that she is not in love with Denisov, yet finds him dear and pities him; accordingly, “love” here denotes continuing personal affection rather than reciprocal romantic love or acceptance of marriage. The utterance reassures Denisov and preserves their bond while explicitly withholding the relationship he requested.
