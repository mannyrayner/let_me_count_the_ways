Roxane accepts that he loves her but demands an eloquent account of how he loves her; thus the comic failure concerns his inability to elaborate, not the semantic force of this avowal. The exclamation mark and romantic intensity do not independently establish expressive/reflexive force, while the courtship setting alone does not make the utterance a relational undertaking.
