Ambiguity noted by the annotator

French « aimer » here may denote strong personal liking or regard rather than romantic love, and the avowal may also serve to ingratiate the marquis with Pirard before the employment offer. Those qualifications do not alter its dominant truth-conditional force.

The marquis moves from « Je vous estime » to the stronger « je vous aime », despite having just met Pirard, after being impressed by his clear and useful legal work. The immediate offer of a highly paid secretaryship gives the avowal a strategic and affiliative setting, but the core words still function as an assertion of personal affection or strong liking. The proposed employment terms and promise to preserve Pirard's parish are separate practical assurances rather than commitment force inherent in « je vous aime ».
