How the scores were assigned

Birkin’s repeated whisper conventionally avows love and is explicitly spoken “with truth,” giving strong T support. Its setting within an already affirmed union gives slight P resonance, but the words do not primarily undertake a new commitment. Nothing independently presents them as a reflex-like discharge, so intensity and gladness do not establish E. Substantial O pressure arises because the narration explicitly treats the I–you love formula as inadequate to Birkin’s intended meaning: a transformation beyond separate selves and even “beyond love.”
