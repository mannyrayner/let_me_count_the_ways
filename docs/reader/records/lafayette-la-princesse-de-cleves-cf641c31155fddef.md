Her impatience and agitation did not allow her to remain with the queen; she went home, although it was not the hour at which she was accustomed to retire. She held the letter in a trembling hand; her thoughts were so confused that she had no distinct one, and she found herself suffering a kind of unbearable pain which she did not know and had never felt before. As soon as she was in her private chamber, she opened the letter and found it to read as follows:

> LETTER

> “I have loved you too much to allow you to believe that the change you perceive in me is an effect of my inconstancy; I want you to know that your infidelity is its cause. You are greatly surprised that I speak to you of your infidelity; you had concealed it from me with such skill, and I took such care to conceal from you that I knew of it, that you have good reason to be astonished that it is known to me. I myself am surprised that I was able to keep any sign of it from appearing to you. No pain has ever equaled mine. I believed that you had a violent passion for me; I no longer concealed from you the passion I had for you, and at the very time when I allowed you to see it in its entirety, I learned that you were deceiving me, that you loved another, and that, to all appearances, you were sacrificing me to this new mistress. I learned it on the day of the ring-tilting tournament; that was why I did not go. I pretended to be ill in order to conceal the disorder of my mind; but I did in fact become ill, and my body could not withstand so violent an agitation. When I began to recover, I continued to pretend that I was very ill, so as to have a pretext for neither seeing you nor writing to you. I wanted time to decide how I should conduct myself toward you; twenty times I made and abandoned the same resolutions; but at last I found you unworthy of seeing my pain, and I resolved not to let it appear to you. I wanted to wound your pride by making you see that my passion was weakening of its own accord. I believed that in this way I would diminish the value of the sacrifice you were making of it; I did not want you to have the pleasure of showing how much I loved you in order to appear more lovable because of it. I resolved to write you cold and languishing letters, so as to plant in the mind of the woman to whom you gave them the thought that you were ceasing to be loved. I did not want her to have the pleasure of learning that I knew she was triumphing over me, nor to increase her triumph by my despair and my reproaches. I thought that I would not punish you enough by breaking with you, and that I would cause you only slight pain if I ceased to love you when you no longer loved me. I found that you had to love me in order to feel the pain of not being loved, which I was experiencing so cruelly. I believed that if anything could rekindle the feelings you had had for me, it was to make you see that mine had changed; but to make you see it while pretending to conceal it from you, and as though I had not had the strength to admit it to you. I settled upon this resolution; but how difficult it was for me to make, and how impossible it seemed to carry out when I saw you again! A hundred times I was on the point of bursting out with reproaches and tears; the state in which my health still left me helped me to disguise my agitation and affliction from you. I was then sustained by the pleasure of dissembling with you as you dissembled with me; nevertheless, I did myself such great violence in order to tell you and write to you that I loved you that you saw sooner than I had intended to let you see that my feelings had changed. You were wounded by it; you complained of it. I tried to reassure you; but it was in so forced a manner that you were even more thoroughly convinced that I no longer loved you. At last, I did everything I had intended to do. The capriciousness of your heart made you return to me as you saw me drawing away from you. I have enjoyed all the pleasure vengeance can give; it seemed to me that you loved me more than you had ever done, and I made you see that I no longer loved you.
