Her emotional and hesitant delivery does not independently establish expressive/reflexive force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Natasha has just told her mother that she is not in love with Denisov, yet finds him dear and pities him; accordingly, “love” here denotes continuing personal affection rather than reciprocal romantic love or acceptance of marriage. The utterance reassures Denisov and preserves their bond while explicitly withholding the relationship he requested.
