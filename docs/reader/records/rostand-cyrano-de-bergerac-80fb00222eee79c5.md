## Original passage

> CHRISTIAN:
>   Je t'aime tant.


## Original AI explanation

Christian’s direct “Je t’aime tant” (“I love you so much”) is principally an avowal presenting his love for Roxane as true. It neither undertakes a distinct commitment nor functions as a reflex-like affective discharge: its repetition results from his inability to elaborate on the avowal as Roxane requests.

## Ambiguity

None recorded.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Christian has rejected Cyrano’s assistance and is trying to speak for himself. His avowal appears sincere but rhetorically impoverished: Roxane accepts love as the established “theme” and asks him to explain how he loves her, while Christian can only intensify and repeat the basic assertion. The comic failure concerns eloquence and courtship performance, not the semantic force of the love claim.
