## Original AI explanation

Jane’s utterance is principally a reflective avowal that her love for Rochester is now stronger, specifically because his changed circumstances allow her to care for and assist him. The surrounding acceptance of marriage gives it slight commitment-reinforcing force, but the sentence itself chiefly presents her emotional state as true. Nothing indicates that the words are a reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Ambiguity

There is limited T/P ambiguity because the avowal occurs during their renewed engagement and supports Jane’s undertaking to become Rochester’s wife, but its comparative and explanatory form remains predominantly truth-conditional.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rochester fears that his blindness, disability, and dependence make marriage to him a sacrifice for Jane. She counters this by avowing that she loves him more now that she can be useful to him than when his pride confined him to the role of giver and protector. The statement reassures him and supports their prospective marriage, while also articulating Jane’s preference for a more reciprocal relationship.
