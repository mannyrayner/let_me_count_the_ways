Johannes privately avows that he still loves Victoria, strongly supporting T. The self-directed whisper, outstretched hands, and immediate repetition also give moderate independent support to E: the words function partly as an affective discharge when he is no longer speaking with her. Nothing indicates a commitment or relational undertaking, so P is unsupported.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The declaration follows a guarded, wounded exchange in which Johannes and Victoria separate without openly acknowledging their attachment. Once she is walking away, Johannes contradicts his outward bitterness in a private avowal of continuing love. His reaching toward her and repeating the phrase suggest emotional overflow, but he does not use the declaration to undertake a commitment or alter their relationship; he calls only her name aloud afterward.
