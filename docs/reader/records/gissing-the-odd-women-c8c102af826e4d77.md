## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Barfoot makes the avowal while defending the seriousness of his earlier conduct and immediately before renewing his marriage proposal. Rhoda doubts both his seriousness and the sincerity of his love, and his later decision to marry Agnes reinforces the possibility that this is self-serving, unstable, or partly self-deceived. Nevertheless, the highlighted words themselves remain a retrospective assertion of love, not a commitment; the adjacent proposal carries the undertaking force.
