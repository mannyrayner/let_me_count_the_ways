## Original AI explanation

Armand’s “Si vous saviez comme je vous aime!” is principally an avowal presenting the intensity of his love for Marguerite as true. Although it occurs during courtship and contributes to their negotiation of a liaison, the words themselves do not undertake a commitment; explicit promises follow separately. The low voice, physical intimacy, excitement, and exclamation mark indicate passionate delivery but do not independently make the utterance an expressive/reflex-like discharge.
