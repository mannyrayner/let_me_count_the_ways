## Original AI explanation

Victoria’s retrospective written avowal presents her enduring past love for Johannes as true. It neither undertakes a future relational commitment nor functions primarily as a reflex-like discharge: the letter is deliberately composed to tell him what she could not previously show.

## Ambiguity

The invocation “Du gode Gud” and the deathbed intensity make the delivery highly expressive in an ordinary sense, but the passage supplies no independent evidence that the core love words are an affective reflex rather than an emotionally charged avowal.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Facing death, Victoria abandons her former shame and reserve and explicitly tells Johannes that she has loved him. She describes the letter as a way of being near him at the end, while also insisting that everything is now too late and that she has no right to summon him. These motives and the intense deathbed framing deepen the avowal but do not turn it into a relational undertaking or independently establish reflexive E-force.
