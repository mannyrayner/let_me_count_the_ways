## Original AI explanation

Loredana’s written «ti amerò sempre» strongly presents her enduring love for Filippo as true. Its future form and farewell-note setting also give it substantial undertaking force: she pledges continuing love and attachment despite leaving him. Nothing indicates that the words are a reflex-like affective discharge.

## Ambiguity

The future «amerò» can operate both as a prediction or avowal of enduring feeling and as a promise-like commitment; the surrounding «sarò tua» strengthens the latter without eliminating the former.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Loredana is separating herself from Filippo as a self-sacrifice intended to save him and protect his ailing mother, not because her love has ended. The note reassures him that apparent distance will not alter her love or belonging to him. Her later explicit statement «Io amo Filippo» corroborates its sincerity, while the planned separation gives the future declaration a pledge-like relational force.
