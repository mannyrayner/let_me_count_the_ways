## Original AI explanation

Pierre’s original “Je vous aime” presents love as a true state and thus has maximal truth-conditional force even though he retrospectively judges it false. In its courtship setting it also helps bind him to Hélène and advance the relationship toward marriage, giving it substantial undertaking force. The text directly negates expressive/reflexive force by describing the words as not coming from within him.

## Ambiguity

The degree of performative force is somewhat uncertain: the words are not an explicit promise or proposal, but Pierre treats saying them as an act that bound him relationally and led into marriage.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Pierre retrospectively interprets the avowal as “a lie and worse than a lie”: he married Hélène without loving her and believes the words deceived both her and himself. Their insincerity does not diminish their truth-conditional mode, while his description of having bound himself through them supports performative force. The remembered utterance is socially consequential but not an affective discharge.
