## Original AI explanation

The highlighted occurrence is Roxane’s direct “je vous aime” within the fuller phrase “Oh ! je vous aime bien.” It avows fondness or affectionate friendship toward Cyrano. Neither the phrase nor its setting independently enacts a relational commitment, and the initial “Oh !” does not by itself establish expressive/reflex-like force.

## Ambiguity

The exact match is embedded in “je vous aime bien,” whose idiomatic force is closer to “I am very fond of you” than an unqualified romantic “I love you.” A separate “Oh ! je vous aime !” occurs shortly afterward but is not the highlighted occurrence.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Roxane says this immediately after Cyrano promises to protect Christian, the man she romantically loves. Her words express affectionate gratitude and confirm her “tender friendship” with Cyrano, not romantic love for him. The context is painfully ironic because Cyrano loves Roxane, but that narrative asymmetry does not alter the utterance’s predominantly truth-conditional force.
