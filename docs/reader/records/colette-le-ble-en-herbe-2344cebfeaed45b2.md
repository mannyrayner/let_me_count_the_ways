## Original passage

> —-Oh! Phil, je t'aime toujours. Malheureusement, ça n'y change rien.


## Original AI explanation

Vinca directly avows that she still loves Philippe. The temporal adverb « toujours » emphasizes the continuing truth of her emotional state, while « Malheureusement, ça n'y change rien » explicitly separates that state from forgiveness, reconciliation, or any renewed relational undertaking. Her gentle, controlled delivery supplies no independent evidence that the words are a reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal answers Philippe's fear that Vinca no longer loves him, but it is concessive rather than reassuring: she maintains that her love persists while insisting that it changes nothing about « nous ». The subsequent discussion of their former expectation of marriage and her wish to become a child again indicates a rupture or transformation that love alone cannot repair. Philippe partially receives her words as absolution, but the narration stresses both her sincere tone and her verbal reticence. These complications concern the consequences and interpretation of the avowal, not its core truth-conditional force.
