## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Lear has explicitly invited his daughters to compete in professing love, with larger territorial rewards attached to the strongest answer. Goneril responds with elaborately patterned hyperbole, claiming that her love exceeds words, eyesight, liberty, and life. The incentivised public setting and Cordelia’s skeptical asides frame the declaration as calculated or unreliable, but deception is compatible with a strongly truth-conditional utterance type. Nothing in the passage presents the words as involuntarily blurted or affectively forced.
