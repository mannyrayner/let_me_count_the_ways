## Original AI explanation

Jean directly avows love and reinforces the assertion with “without doubt—can you doubt it?” The utterance does not itself undertake a commitment, and its hesitant, responsive, controlled delivery supplies no independent evidence of expressive/reflexive discharge.

## Ambiguity

Jean’s sincerity and possible strategic motives remain uncertain, but those issues do not alter the utterance’s strongly truth-conditional mode. The surrounding escape plan creates relational implications without making this sentence an undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Miss Julie solicits the declaration as reassurance and courage for their proposed flight. Jean answers affirmatively but hesitates, declines immediate physical intimacy, retains the formal pronoun “er,” and stresses the barriers imposed by the house and the Count. His later promise to repeat the words elsewhere and his insistence on suppressing feelings suggest a controlled and potentially strategic avowal rather than a reflex-like outburst. Whether his professed love is sincere is narratively questionable but orthogonal to its assertoric force.
