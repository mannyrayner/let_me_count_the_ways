## Original AI explanation

Mary embeds the loving-state proposition in a hypothetical antecedent rather than avowing it as true. The utterance neither undertakes a relationship nor functions as an affective verbal discharge; indeed, it explicitly separates possible love from any promise of marriage.

## Ambiguity

The counterfactual-sounding wording may pragmatically suggest that Mary denies loving Fred, or may allow her to conceal feelings, but it does not assert either interpretation.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Fred asks Mary to say that she loves him and promise marriage. Mary refuses the requested encouragement by arguing that even love would not justify marrying an idle, indebted man. Her formulation is therefore a hypothetical rebuttal to Fred’s attempt to connect affection with marital obligation, not a straightforward confession. Her reddening and Fred’s belief that she cares may create interpersonal ambiguity, but they do not change the core hypothetical force.
