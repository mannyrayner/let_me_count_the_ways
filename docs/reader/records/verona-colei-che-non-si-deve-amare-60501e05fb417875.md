## Original AI explanation

Rafa’s embedded “ti amerei” occurs under negation in the counterfactual construction “altrimenti non ti amerei” (“otherwise I would not love you”). In context, this reasoning presents his present love for Loretta as true and as grounded partly in profound respect. It neither undertakes a commitment nor functions as an affect-driven verbal discharge.

## Ambiguity

Loretta disputes Rafa’s characterization, arguing that he desires rather than loves her; this raises uncertainty about sincerity or self-understanding, not about the utterance’s strongly truth-conditional force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rafa uses the claim to reassure Loretta and rebut her suggestion that he considers her frivolous. Loretta immediately rejects his avowal and contrasts love with mere desire, citing his conduct in bringing her there. The exchange may involve courtship or persuasion, but the core expression still presents Rafa’s love as an existing fact rather than enacting a relational undertaking. Nothing indicates reflex-like or uncontrolled production.
