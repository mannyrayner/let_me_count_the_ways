## Original AI explanation

Christian’s direct “Je t’aime” is principally an avowal presenting his love for Roxane as true. It neither articulates nor independently enacts a commitment, and the deliberate conversational setting supplies no positive evidence that the words are a reflex-like affective discharge.

## Ambiguity

The declaration occurs within courtship, but courtship and a desire for reciprocation do not by themselves establish performative undertaking force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Christian has refused Cyrano’s coaching because he wants to speak for himself, then answers Roxane’s invitation to speak with a blunt declaration of love. Roxane treats it as merely the basic theme and demands elaboration; Christian’s subsequent repetitions reveal limited eloquence rather than expressive/reflexive force. His later request that Roxane say she loves him shows a desire for reciprocation, not a commitment encoded by the highlighted utterance.
