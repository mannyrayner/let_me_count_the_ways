## Original AI explanation

It does not itself undertake a commitment, and the surrounding passion and trembling do not establish an independently expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rhoda sincerely presents her love as real while remaining uncertain about the practical commitment Everard demands. Her refusal to decide immediately, kiss him, or accept the symbolic ring separates this avowal of feeling from an undertaking to marry or enter his proposed union. The narration confirms her passion, but that intensity is contextual evidence and does not make the words a reflex-like discharge.
