## Original AI explanation

Nora directly avows as true that she has loved Torvald above everything in the world. The utterance retrospectively presents her enduring love as the motive or meaning behind her conduct; it does not undertake a new commitment, and there is no positive evidence that the words are a reflex-like expressive discharge.

Her avowal frames her incriminating action as arising from profound love for Torvald and occurs while she expects him to assume the burden for her; moments later, his self-protective response exposes the mismatch between her understanding of their marriage and his.
