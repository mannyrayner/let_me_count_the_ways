## Original AI explanation

Birkin’s “I love you” is primarily a sincere avowal of love and also participates in his acceptance of Ursula as his partner and in their imminent marriage. Its whispering, physical intensity, and trembling establish passionate delivery but do not independently make the words an expressive/reflex-like discharge.

## Ambiguity

The narration says that the conventional formula inadequately expresses Birkin’s experience of transformed, impersonal union. This complicates what his avowal means, but does not establish a distinct core force outside T and P.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal occurs after Birkin tells Ursula that she will stay with him and that they are already effectively married. The narration presents his words as truthful and deeply meant, associating them with gratitude, renewed life, and acceptance of Ursula as his mate. Ursula nevertheless wants more explicit proof, while the narrator stresses that “I love you” cannot fully communicate Birkin’s vision of their transcendent oneness. That representational inadequacy concerns the extraordinary state behind the utterance rather than requiring another category of core speech-act force.
