## Original AI explanation

Christian’s “Je vous aime” is a direct, unqualified avowal presenting his love for Roxane as true. It neither undertakes a specific relational commitment nor exhibits positive evidence of reflex-like expressive discharge; the silence and emotional setting do not independently establish E.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Christian has rejected Cyrano’s offer to prepare or supply his speech and intends to speak for himself. His bare declaration is therefore framed as his own sincere avowal, but Roxane finds it rhetorically inadequate and repeatedly asks him to elaborate. Her dissatisfaction concerns eloquence, not the intelligibility of his assertion of love. Although the scene is courtship and Christian hopes for reciprocation, this particular utterance does not itself establish a commitment or undertaking.
