## Original AI explanation

It does not undertake a relational commitment: indeed, he insists that poverty makes their belonging to each other impossible. Although the scene and delivery are intensely emotional, the words remain an avowal rather than an independently evidenced reflex-like discharge.

## Ambiguity

The clause is embedded in a concessive hypothetical (“Even if you loved me as well as I love you”), but Will’s own love is treated as the factual baseline rather than as hypothetical.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Will confesses his love while arguing that mutual love would still be insufficient to overcome his poverty and their apparent inability to marry. His failure to maintain his intended silence and the passionate, storm-framed encounter establish emotional intensity, but not a separate expressive/reflexive force. The utterance avows love while his surrounding speech rejects hope of a viable union rather than committing him to one.
