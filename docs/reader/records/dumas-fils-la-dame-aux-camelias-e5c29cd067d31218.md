## Original AI explanation

Marguerite’s “rappelez-vous seulement que je vous aime” (“just remember that I love you”) explicitly presents her love for Armand as an enduring emotional state he should treat as true. It supports her attempt to reassure him and secure his agreement, but the core utterance does not itself substantially undertake a commitment. Its deliberate, reason-giving formulation supplies no independent expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The avowal has a persuasive function within Marguerite’s argument, but persuasion and reassurance do not by themselves make the love utterance performative.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Marguerite is trying to overcome Armand’s jealousy and suspicion about her undisclosed financial scheme. She invokes her love as a reason for him to trust her, stop worrying, and consent to their proposed summer together. This strategic and reassuring use does not displace the utterance’s dominant force as an avowal of an emotional state; nor does the surrounding intensity (“j’ai la folie de vous aimer”) make the highlighted sentence an expressive discharge.
