## Original AI explanation

“Je vous aimais” occurs inside the counterfactual condition “si je vous aimais” (“if I loved you”). It introduces a loving state proposition but neither avows nor presents that state as actually true; in context it strongly implies the opposite. There is no relational undertaking and no expressive/reflex-like discharge.

## Ambiguity

T receives slight support because ordinary love-state content is truth-conditionally represented within the conditional, although it is not asserted and is pragmatically rejected.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

De Guiche is responding to Cyrano’s accusation of revenge. His courtly counterfactual is caustic rather than affectionate: he effectively disclaims love while admitting that rancor helped motivate his decision. He also credits the Gascons’ unmatched bravery and claims that sacrificing them serves the King. The love wording therefore structures an ironic counterfactual contrast, not an avowal of affection.
