## Original AI explanation

Victoria’s written “jeg har elsket Dem” (“I have loved you”) is an explicit retrospective avowal presented as true and intensified by “loved only you throughout my entire life.” It does not itself undertake a future commitment. Although written under extreme emotion near death, the letter is deliberate and repeatedly resumed, so the evidence supports passionate truth-conditional avowal rather than an independently reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Victoria finally discloses a lifelong love that she says she had previously been unable to show. The impending final separation, her desire to feel near Johannes, her request for forgiveness, and the invocation of God as witness heighten the avowal’s solemnity and credibility. Her explicit reflection on why she is writing and her repeated returns to the letter indicate purposeful confession, not an unmediated verbal reflex.
