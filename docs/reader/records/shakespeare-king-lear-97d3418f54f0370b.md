## Original passage

## Original AI explanation

Cordelia directly avows that she loves Lear, making truth-conditional force dominant. “According to my bond” also invokes the filial obligation structuring that love, giving substantial performative support, though she affirms an existing bond rather than undertaking a new commitment. Her controlled refusal to turn her heart into theatrical speech provides no independent expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The main uncertainty is the strength of P: “according to my bond” invokes filial obligation, but the utterance primarily describes the love and duty Cordelia already bears rather than creating or renewing them.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Lear demands a public declaration that will determine Cordelia’s inheritance. Cordelia rejects her sisters’ expansive rhetoric and offers a deliberately bounded avowal: she loves Lear truly, but only in the measure proper to a daughter’s bond, leaving room for the future claims of a husband. The coercive and transactional setting explains the speech’s restraint without changing its core T/P force.
