## Original AI explanation

Emma’s “Je t’aime!” is principally a direct avowal of her love for Rodolphe and also explains her impulsive visit. Its enactment of commitment is weak: the affair already includes vows, but this sentence does not itself clearly undertake a new obligation. The unexpected substitution of a love declaration for a practical answer gives slight support to affective overflow, though the utterance remains adequately understood mainly as an emotionally intense avowal.

## Ambiguity

The exclamation and embrace do not independently establish E. Slight E support comes instead from the declaration’s affect-driven, pragmatically indirect response to Rodolphe’s questions; it could still be interpreted entirely as an emphatic T avowal.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Emma is exhilarated by the newly consummated affair and models her experience partly on the adulterous heroines of her reading. Her declaration appears sincere as an avowal of her present infatuation, while also operating as an indirect explanation of why longing drove her to make the risky dawn visit. The surrounding vows and established lover relationship provide limited commitment-related coloring, but the sentence itself does not clearly renew or formulate an undertaking. Narrative irony about Emma’s romantic scripts and Rodolphe’s later annoyance complicates the relationship without changing the utterance’s dominant T force.
