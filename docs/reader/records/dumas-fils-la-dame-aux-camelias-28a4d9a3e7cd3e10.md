## Original AI explanation

Armand’s “Parce que je vous aime, pardieu!” presents his alleged love for Olympe as the reason for his proposition, so its core force is a truth-conditional avowal even though the narrative establishes that it is knowingly false. The love clause itself does not undertake a commitment, and its emphatic delivery supplies no independent evidence of expressive/reflexive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The utterance occurs within a proposal to become Olympe’s lover, but the relational and transactional undertaking is made by the surrounding offer rather than by the love avowal itself.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal is a calculated pretext in a financially explicit sexual bargain. Olympe immediately identifies Armand’s real motive as revenge against Marguerite, and his retrospective narration confirms that he loves Marguerite while feeling disgust toward Olympe. The deception affects sincerity and truth, not the utterance’s truth-conditional mode. “Pardieu!” adds emphatic insistence but does not make the words a reflex-like affective discharge.
