## Original AI explanation

The flower directly avows that she loves the little prince and explains that her previous conduct prevented him from knowing it. The words disclose a loving state rather than undertake a new relational commitment. Their calm, deliberate placement within an apology and explanation provides no independent evidence of expressive/reflexive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The farewell context makes the avowal an enactment of tenderness in a broad sense, but the core words neither promise continued commitment nor appear reflexively produced, so this does not materially support P or E.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

At the moment of the little prince’s departure, the proud flower abandons her customary reproaches, apologizes, and explicitly reveals affection that her evasions had concealed. « Mais oui » gives the avowal a corrective force: he failed to recognize her love because she communicated it badly. Her subsequent concern for his happiness and concealment of her tears support sincerity and tenderness, while her composed explanation weighs against treating the words as a reflex-like emotional outburst.
