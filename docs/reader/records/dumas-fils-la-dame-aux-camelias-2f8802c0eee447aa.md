## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

After learning of Armand’s conflict with his father, Marguerite argues that the father should be pleased she is Armand’s mistress because she loves him and seeks no more than his means permit. The avowal therefore has a reason-giving and reassuring role. Her concern about separating Armand from his family, their arrangements for the future, and her later assurance that she will remain his provide evidence of sincere attachment, but they do not turn this particular clause into a commitment-performing or reflexive utterance.
