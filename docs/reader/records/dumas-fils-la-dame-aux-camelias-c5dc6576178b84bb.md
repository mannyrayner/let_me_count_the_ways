Her embrace, tears, and emphatic delivery are adequately treated as context for a passionate assertion; they do not independently establish expressive/reflexive force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Marguerite says this after asking Armand never to leave her and scrutinizing the sincerity of his oath. Her avowal explains the stakes of her fear: she experiences their shared life as transformative and dreads abandonment and a return to her former existence. The surrounding embrace and concealment of her face intensify the sincerity and vulnerability of the declaration without changing its dominant truth-conditional force.
