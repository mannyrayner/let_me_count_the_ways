## Original AI explanation

Cyrano’s “je vous aime” is foremost a sincere avowal presenting his love for Roxane as true. It also has independently supported expressive/reflexive force: he announces that he will cast out whatever words come to him without arranging them, and the declaration emerges amid suffocation, agitation, and loss of verbal control. No relational undertaking is enacted by the core words themselves.

## Ambiguity

The degree of E is somewhat interpretive: the highly wrought verse remains a lucid avowal, but the explicit framing as an unarranged outpouring supplies more than mere passionate delivery. Cyrano’s concealed identity complicates attribution for Roxane but not the T/P/E force of his own utterance.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Cyrano contrasts this speech with his former artificial wit and says that he is finally speaking sincerely from his “true heart.” The declaration expresses his own love, although Roxane takes the hidden speaker to be Christian. Her response—“that is indeed love”—confirms its received force as an avowal, while the subsequent claim that he would sacrifice his happiness for hers elaborates the character of the feeling rather than turning the highlighted words into a commitment.
