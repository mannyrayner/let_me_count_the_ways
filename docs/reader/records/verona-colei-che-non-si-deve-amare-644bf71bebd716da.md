Rafa’s “Come ti amo, Loretta!” (“How I love you, Loretta!”) strongly avows the extent of his loving state. Its immediate emergence in response to Loretta’s hint of reciprocal attraction, together with the narrator’s description of his ingenuous exclamation, gives moderate independent support to expressive discharge. It does not itself undertake a commitment or obligation.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rafa has already repeatedly described his love and pressed Loretta not to end their meetings. When she hints that she may herself be in danger of no longer treating the affair lightly, he immediately answers with this exclamation and puts an arm around her waist. The utterance therefore both reaffirms his established emotional claim and reacts affectively to apparent encouragement. Although the wider conversation concerns continuing their relationship, these words contain no distinct promise or undertaking.
