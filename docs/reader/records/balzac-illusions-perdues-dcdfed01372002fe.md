## Original AI explanation

Ève’s “Je vous aime bien...” is principally a direct avowal of her love for David. In this marriage-proposal exchange it also helps enact her acceptance of a future with him, although the explicit undertaking is supplied more fully by her immediately following statement that she loves him enough to become his wife and devote herself to him. Her measured, smiling response provides no independent evidence of expressive/reflexive discharge.

## Ambiguity

Ève’s restrained declaration confirms the feelings she has already described and, within this exchange, contributes to accepting the proposed relationship. The subsequent “Assez pour être votre femme” establishes that her love is sufficient for marriage and self-dedication. The smile, emotion, and romantic setting affect delivery but do not make the highlighted words a reflex-like discharge.
