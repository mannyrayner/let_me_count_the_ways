## Original AI explanation

## Ambiguity

The phrase “as a sister” positions their relationship, but in this argumentative context it qualifies the kind of love asserted rather than enacting a substantial relational undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Jane uses the avowal to explain why she would have risked much by accompanying St. John while firmly rejecting his claim that she is obligated to go, his marriage proposal, and any romantic interpretation of her affection. The sisterly qualification is contrastive and boundary-setting: she cares for and trusts him, but her heart remains attached to Rochester.
