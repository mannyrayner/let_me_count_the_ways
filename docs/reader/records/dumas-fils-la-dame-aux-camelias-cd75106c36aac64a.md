## Original AI explanation

The intensifier “comme un fou” and his emotional posture indicate passion without independently establishing expressive/reflexive force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

After Marguerite explains that financial dependence and surveillance constrain her relations with other men, Armand concedes her point but contrasts it with the extremity of his love. His avowal is sincere-seeming and emotionally intense, while also functioning rhetorically as an explanation of his possessiveness and hurt. Marguerite’s reply—he should have loved her less or understood her better—treats the love as real but insufficiently informed by her circumstances.
