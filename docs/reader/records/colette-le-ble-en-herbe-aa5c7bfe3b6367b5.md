## Original AI explanation

Vinca’s “Je t’aime!” is primarily a desperate avowal presenting her love as true. Its description as part of an emotional eruption and as a cry analogous to “Adieu!” gives it some independently expressive force, though it remains intelligible chiefly as an intensely delivered avowal. The surrounding refusal of separation lends weak commitment-like resonance, but the core words do not clearly undertake a relational obligation.

## Ambiguity

The main uncertainty is whether the farewell comparison establishes genuinely expressive/reflexive force or merely characterizes the intensity and valedictory circumstances of a truth-conditional avowal. Performative force is only weakly suggested by the adjacent claim that she can no longer leave him.

Vinca utters the words amid tears, desperate confessions, and horror at an impending separation. The simile “comme on crie ‘Adieu!’” frames the declaration as a despairing, almost valedictory cry: she avows love precisely when their future appears unreachable and separation intolerable. Her accompanying “Je ne peux plus te quitter!” intensifies the attachment and provides limited commitment-like resonance, while the narration’s account of their lifelong bond supports the avowal’s sincerity.
