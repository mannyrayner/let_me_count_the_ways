## Original AI explanation

The embedded clause “je t’aime” presents Madame de Rênal’s love for Julien as true; her question concerns whether she loves him enough, not whether she loves him at all. The question makes no distinct commitment or relational undertaking. Although emotionally exclaimed, it is an intense request for reassurance about an avowed state, with no independent evidence that the words are a reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The interrogative form somewhat indirectly conveys the avowal, but its presupposition of love is strong. The exclamatory reporting verb indicates intensity without independently supporting E.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Madame de Rênal asks Julien whether he finds that she loves him enough amid an adulterous relationship marked by devotion, sacrifice, remorse, and fear of damnation. Julien’s wounded pride requires visible sacrificial proof of her love, so the question seeks reassurance that her love is sufficiently demonstrated. The surrounding passion and the verb “s’écriait-elle” intensify the avowal but do not turn it into an independently reflexive expression or a commitment.
