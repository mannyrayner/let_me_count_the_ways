## Original AI explanation

The apparent negation is qualified by the comparison: Armand does not deny loving Marguerite but swears that he loves her differently from her previous lovers, implying a more genuine and noncommercial love. This is principally a truth-conditional avowal. Its embedding in “je te jure” gives it some performative force as a solemn assurance and relational pledge, though it does not clearly undertake a specific future obligation. Nothing indicates reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The oath supports some P, but it may be read primarily as strengthening the asserted avowal rather than creating a substantial relational undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Armand uses the avowal to persuade Marguerite to grant him the exceptional privilege of keeping her key. The continuation makes clear that he claims not an absence of love but a love unlike that of her former clients or lovers. Marguerite understands it as evidence that he loves her, and the narrator subsequently frames his happiness as arising from being genuinely loved by a courtesan. The persuasive setting may heighten or strategically deploy the avowal, but it does not alter its core T/P analysis.
