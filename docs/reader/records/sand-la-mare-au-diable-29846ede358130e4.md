## Original AI explanation

The embedded “je t’aime” presents Germain’s love as a true inner state supposedly legible in his eyes. The personification of his eyes emphasizes visible evidence and sincerity, not a commitment undertaken by the words or a reflex-like verbal discharge.

## Ambiguity

The declaration occurs during marriage-oriented courtship, but that context alone does not give the embedded love proposition substantial performative force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Germain is seeking Marie’s response after having been encouraged to ask her to marry him. He portrays his gaze as unmistakable evidence of a love he has already verbally acknowledged. His anxious courtship and the later revelation that Marie reciprocates support a sincere avowal, while the surrounding marriage proposal remains contextual rather than converting this embedded clause into an undertaking.

> Here ends the story of Germain’s marriage, just as he told it to me himself, the shrewd plowman that he is!
