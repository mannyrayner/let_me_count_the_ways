Because it is used while pressing a concrete proposal and seeking her assent, it has slight commitment-invoking force, but the undertaking is chiefly carried by the surrounding promises and plans rather than by “Ti amo” itself. The passionate, supplicatory delivery does not independently establish expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rafa presents the declaration as evidence that Loretta is unique and that his proposed arrangement will not be temporary or serial. It occurs within an ardent attempt to obtain her assent, accompanied by promises, pleading, physical pressure, and disregard of her requests to be released. This gives the avowal a persuasive and potentially manipulative role without displacing its truth-conditional mode; the passage may portray his desire as genuine even while his conduct is coercive.
