## Original AI explanation

Natasha’s repeated “Я люблю тебя” is principally an avowal of love addressed in imagination to the dead Prince Andrei. It also has some undertaking force because it forms part of her corrective, counterfactual assurance that she would bind herself to him and share his suffering. The repetition, tears, and convulsive physical effort make the delivery intensely emotional but do not independently establish expressive/reflexive force: the words remain a contentful avowal within a consciously reconstructed conversation.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Grieving Prince Andrei, Natasha mentally revises an earlier answer that she fears he misunderstood. She imagines telling him that life without him is nothing and that sharing his suffering would have been happiness, then repeats “I love you” as the tender declaration she wishes she had made. The utterance therefore combines retrospective avowal with a corrective assurance of relational devotion. Its anguish and repetition reflect bereavement and irreversible regret, but those delivery features do not require an independent E classification.
