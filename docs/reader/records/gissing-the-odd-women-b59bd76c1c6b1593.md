## Original AI explanation

Bevis directly avows that he loves Monica, intensifying the asserted state with “with all my soul.” The declaration offers emotional reassurance but does not itself undertake the concrete commitment Monica seeks—leaving with her—and his accompanying proposal merely to discuss what can be done delays that decision. The exclamation and subsequent passion show intensity, not independent expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The crisis setting gives the declaration some relational significance, but the text pointedly separates professed love from willingness to undertake the requested commitment.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal is elicited by Monica’s demand for love to be demonstrated through action. Bevis strongly professes love while shifting toward deliberation—“let us talk about it”—and subsequently emphasizes practical risks. Monica experiences this disparity between passionate avowal and concrete resolve as cowardice and disillusionment. His emotional and physical excitement may be genuine, but neither it nor the exclamatory delivery makes the words a reflex-like discharge, and the declaration does not itself promise elopement.
