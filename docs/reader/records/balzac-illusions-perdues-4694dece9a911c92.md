## Original AI explanation

In “vous saurez combien je vous aime,” Lucien presents his existing love for Ève and David as a fact whose degree his future results will reveal. The utterance is therefore strongly truth-conditional. It makes no distinct relational undertaking, and its controlled role in an extended justification supplies no positive evidence of reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The reassurance has a forward-looking, quasi-promissory setting, but the love clause itself does not commit Lucien to a specific obligation or course of conduct.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Lucien embraces Ève and David while defending his decision to leave for Paris despite Ève’s impending wedding and the family’s financial sacrifices. His avowal reassures them and rhetorically reframes ambition and eventual success as proof of love. This potentially self-serving or self-deceptive use affects how the avowal is received, but not its core truth-presenting force. The sadness, kisses, and embraces indicate emotional delivery without independently establishing expressive/reflexive force.
