In this moment of choosing their relationship over the duke’s support, the avowal also helps ratify her relational commitment, giving P moderate support. The murmuring, embrace, emotion, and emphatic “Oh! oui” indicate intensity but do not independently establish reflex-like expressive discharge, so E is unsupported.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Marguerite’s declaration answers Armand’s reciprocal appeal after he overhears her refuse to leave him for the duke’s financial support. Her avowal is corroborated by her stated willingness to sacrifice that support, her plans for a quiet shared life, and her subsequent changes in conduct. The scene frames the utterance as sincere, emotionally intense, and partly relationship-ratifying, while her request that Armand never reproach her past reveals vulnerability and a need for reassurance.
