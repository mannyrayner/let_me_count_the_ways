## Original AI explanation

Marguerite’s “je t’aime” is primarily an explicit avowal of her loving state. It also moderately supports performative force because, in this exchange, it ratifies her choice of Armand and accompanies a concrete undertaking to abandon her former life. The exclamation, murmuring, embrace, and emotion intensify the avowal but do not independently show that the words are a reflex-like affective discharge.

## Ambiguity

The main uncertainty is the degree of P: the surrounding turn strongly commits Marguerite to a shared life, but much of that undertaking is expressed by the following future-tense promises rather than by “je t’aime” alone.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Marguerite answers Armand’s declaration of mutual devotion after he overhears her refusing the duke’s financial support rather than leave him. Her avowal is corroborated by that costly private choice and by her subsequent conduct. In the same turn, she projects a peaceful shared future and vows to leave her former life, giving the exchange some commitment-enacting force. Her murmuring, embrace, and emotional state mark intense delivery but do not establish independent E force.
