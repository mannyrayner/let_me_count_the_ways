## Original passage

## Original AI explanation

Julien directly and deliberately avows as true that he has always loved Mme de Rênal and has loved only her. The retrospective perfect and the framing imperative “Sache que” strongly support T. The statement contains no independent promise or relational undertaking, and the intense emotion surrounding it does not establish expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The tears, kisses, and emotional extremity make the delivery highly passionate, but the text presents the words as an intelligible avowal rather than a reflex-like discharge.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal occurs during Julien and Mme de Rênal's emotionally intense prison reunion after she has forgiven him. It contrasts his enduring love for her with his relationship to Mathilde; his later claim that Mathilde is his wife only “in appearance” reinforces the exclusivity asserted here. The scene strongly supports sincerity, but sincerity and passion remain contextual features rather than separate core forces.
