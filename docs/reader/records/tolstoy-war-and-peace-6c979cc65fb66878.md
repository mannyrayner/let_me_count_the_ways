The utterance is anticipated in his prior reflection and grounded in a carefully articulated conception of divine love, so it is not presented as a reflex-like expressive discharge.

His avowal answers her plea for forgiveness and communicates forgiveness and reconciliation. The subsequent “I love you more, better than before” confirms the avowed state, while the narrator’s statement that neither of them discusses resuming their former engagement limits the case for performative recommitment.
