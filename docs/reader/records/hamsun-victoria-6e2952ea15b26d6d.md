## Original AI explanation

The old woman’s “jeg elsker dig til døden” is principally an unequivocal avowal that her love persists despite her husband’s disfigurement. “Til døden” gives the avowal a weak vow-like implication of lifelong devotion, but the clause does not clearly undertake a new obligation. Her stammering, exclamation, and hand-kissing mark intense delivery without independently making the highlighted clause an expressive/reflexive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The phrase “til døden” can weakly renew marital commitment as well as describe the extremity and endurance of her love; hence limited P support. The affective delivery creates some superficial E pressure, but T plus contextual intensity adequately captures it.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The husband has deliberately disfigured himself and asks whether his wife still loves him. Her answer reassures him by affirming continued attraction and love unto death. It culminates an idealized account of reciprocal marital constancy in which each spouse responds to the other’s disfigurement or disability with sacrificial solidarity. The framing and heightened delivery intensify the avowal but do not displace its central truth-conditional force.
