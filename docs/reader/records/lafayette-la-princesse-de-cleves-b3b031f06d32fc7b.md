## Original AI explanation

“Je vous aimais” is a direct, retrospective avowal that Monsieur de Clèves loved his wife, further specifying that his love made him willing to remain deceived. It strongly presents a past emotional state as true. It neither undertakes a new relational commitment nor functions independently as a reflex-like discharge; the emotional intensity belongs to the delivery and surrounding reproach.

## Ambiguity

The imperfect tense presents the love as past or no longer intact, consistent with his claim that she has taken away his esteem and tenderness; this temporal qualification does not alter the truth-conditional force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal forms part of a sustained deathbed reproach. Monsieur de Clèves contrasts the depth and legitimacy of his former love with what he believes to be his wife's betrayal and with Nemours's allegedly seductive intentions. His jealousy and mistaken suspicions shape the accusation but do not change the core clause's status as an avowal of past love. Although the narration says his conflicting feelings could not remain contained, the highlighted clause itself is reflective and explanatory—“je l’avoue à ma honte”—rather than a reflex-like verbal eruption.
