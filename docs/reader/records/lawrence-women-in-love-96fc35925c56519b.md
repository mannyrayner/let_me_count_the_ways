## Original AI explanation

Birkin’s repeated whisper conventionally avows love and is explicitly spoken “with truth,” giving strong T support. Its setting within an already affirmed union gives slight P resonance, but the words do not primarily undertake a new commitment. Nothing independently presents them as a reflex-like discharge, so intensity and gladness do not establish E. Substantial O pressure arises because the narration explicitly treats the I–you love formula as inadequate to Birkin’s intended meaning: a transformation beyond separate selves and even “beyond love.”

## Ambiguity

The principal ambiguity is whether the transcendent meaning should be treated as an unusually metaphysical loving-state avowal within T or as genuinely residual core force under O.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Birkin and Ursula have already treated themselves as effectively married and will marry legally the next day. Ursula hears an insufficiently demonstrative declaration, whereas the narration presents Birkin’s words as a truthful but radically inadequate formula for gratitude, rebirth, and a paradisal union in which separate “I” and “you” are transcended. Thus the interpersonal reception and the narrated intended meaning diverge sharply.
