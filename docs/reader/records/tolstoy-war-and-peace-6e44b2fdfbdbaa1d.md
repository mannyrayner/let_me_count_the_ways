## Original AI explanation

Its intimate relational setting and their prior bond give it slight commitment-invoking force, but it does not itself clearly promise or undertake a future relationship. Despite intense feeling, it is a considered avowal anticipated by explicit reflection, not an expressive or reflex-like verbal discharge.

## Ambiguity

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Before speaking, he silently formulates that he loves her more than anything and wonders what to do about it; his spoken declaration then leads into a question about whether he will live. This supports a sincere, reflective avowal rather than a spontaneous affective discharge. Natasha receives it joyfully, but the scene remains dominated by mortality and does not establish an explicit new commitment to a shared future.
