## Original AI explanation

Emma directly avows her maternal love for Berthe. The exclamative construction “Comme je t’aime!”, its immediate repetition, and its emergence during a sudden rush to embrace the child also give the words substantial expressive-discharge force. They do not undertake a commitment or obligation.

## Ambiguity

The E score is somewhat calibration-sensitive: the passage can also be read as an intensely delivered avowal, but the syntactic exclamation and narration of a sudden, excessive outpouring provide positive evidence beyond intensity alone.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

After her father’s letter evokes childhood happiness and makes her confront her present misery, Emma hears Berthe laughing and abruptly redirects intense affection toward her. She rushes to embrace, kiss, clean, and question the child while shedding tears. The servant’s astonishment at this “excès de tendresse” frames the episode as an unusually sudden outpouring, while the subsequent narrative suggests that this maternal revival may be unstable or compensatory; that instability does not negate the utterance’s avowal force.
