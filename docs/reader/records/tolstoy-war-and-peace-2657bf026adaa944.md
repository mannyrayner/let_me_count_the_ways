## Original AI explanation

Prince Vassily’s embedded «я люблю вас» presents his affection for Anna Mikhailovna as true, specifically as a state to be demonstrated by granting her request. The love clause is therefore strongly truth-conditional even if socially strategic or insincere. It weakly invokes their relational history and obligation, but the explicit undertaking lies chiefly in the accompanying promise to secure her son’s transfer, not in the love avowal itself. Nothing indicates reflex-like expressive discharge.

## Ambiguity

The avowal may be conventional, exaggerated, or strategically deployed rather than sincere; this affects its contextual interpretation, not its truth-conditional mode.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

The avowal helps frame a reluctant concession as evidence of affection and respect for Anna Mikhailovna’s late father. Narration emphasizes Vassily’s boredom and practical calculation: conscience and his wish to avoid continued pestering sway him. Thus the phrase is socially strategic and possibly conventional, while still presenting love as a fact. His concrete promise supplies the scene’s principal commitment force but should not be conflated with the core love clause.
