## Original passage

> RANK (reiser seg). At jeg har elsket Dem fullt så inderlig
> som noen annen? Var det stygt?


## Original AI explanation

Rank’s rhetorical question reiterates and explicitly avows that he has loved Nora as deeply as anyone else has. Its core force is strongly truth-conditional: the interrogative framing challenges Nora’s judgment rather than casting doubt on his love. It does not itself undertake a commitment, and nothing indicates that the words are a reflex-like affective discharge.

## Ambiguity

The perfect form can sound retrospective, but the surrounding confession and Rank’s continuing offer of devotion present the love as enduring and currently relevant.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Rank has just indirectly disclosed his love by saying he would gladly give his life for Nora and had sworn that she should know before he died. After Nora calls his conduct horrid, he reformulates the disclosure explicitly: the objection, he asks, cannot be that he has loved her deeply. Nora confirms that the problem is not his feeling but his telling her, because the disclosure destroys the uncomplicated friendship on which she had intended to rely. His declaration therefore functions principally as a sincere avowal, despite its relational consequences and his accompanying offers of total assistance.
