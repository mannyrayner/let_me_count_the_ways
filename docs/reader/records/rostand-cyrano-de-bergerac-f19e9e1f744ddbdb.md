## Original AI explanation

“Je vous aime” is a deliberate written avowal presenting Cyrano’s love as true. The phrase itself does not undertake a commitment or obligation, and the surrounding courtship situation alone does not establish performative force. Its careful prior composition and present transcription strongly oppose a reflex-like expressive reading.

## Ambiguity

The words are both written into the letter and audibly murmured, but the murmuring revoices the written avowal rather than constituting a distinct reflexive outburst.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Cyrano is too afraid to voice his feelings directly and plans to deliver a love letter instead. He describes the letter as having been composed and recomposed internally a hundred times and as now merely being copied, making the utterance highly deliberate despite his nervous emotional state. Its intended delivery makes it a consequential act of courtship, but the core words remain an avowal rather than an explicit relational undertaking.
