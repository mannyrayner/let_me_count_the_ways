## Original AI explanation

Birkin directly avows as true that he has loved Gerald. The perfect construction presents love as a state extending through some prior interval, while “don’t forget” turns the avowal into a bitter reminder or reproach. It does not itself undertake a future commitment, and the bitter delivery supplies no independent evidence of expressive/reflex-like discharge.

## Ambiguity

Gerald immediately questions whether Birkin truly loved him, creating uncertainty about sincerity or self-knowledge rather than about the utterance’s predominantly truth-conditional force. The elliptical scope of “as well as Gudrun” is somewhat ambiguous but does not alter the core classification.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Birkin interrupts Gerald’s entranced account of his destructive relationship with Gudrun by bitterly reminding him of Birkin’s own love. The utterance asserts a neglected emotional fact and implicitly reproaches Gerald for overlooking their bond. Gerald’s icy response—“Have you? Or do you think you have?”—challenges the avowal’s truth or Birkin’s self-understanding, but does not convert it into a performative undertaking or expressive reflex.
