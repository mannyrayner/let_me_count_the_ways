## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Elisabet explicitly contrasts marriage for love with her own proposed marriage of sacrifice for her sick child: “det är inte så med mig” precedes the conditional, and she says that if she loved Gösta she would not dare make this request. The passage therefore uses the love proposition counterfactually to disclaim romantic motivation. The later narration attributes love to Gösta, not Elisabet, and should not be transferred to her utterance.
