## Original AI explanation

“I have loved none but you” is primarily an avowal presented as true: Wentworth asserts the exclusivity and continuity of his love. It also contributes to his renewed offer of himself and invokes relational constancy, giving it moderate performative support, though the sentence itself is not an explicit promise. Despite the letter’s intense and hurried composition, the words are formulated as an assertion rather than a reflex-like affective discharge.

The sentence appears within Wentworth’s urgent written renewal of his suit.
