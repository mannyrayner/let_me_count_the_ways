## Original AI explanation

Pierre’s “Я никогда не перестаю тебя любить” (“I never stop loving you”) directly avows the uninterrupted persistence of his love. Its core force is truth-conditional: he distinguishes his constant love from the especially renewed feeling caused by separation. The wording does not itself undertake a new commitment, and its reflective, corrective delivery supplies no independent expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The marital setting and reassuring effect create slight pragmatic pressure toward P, but the core wording presents continuity of feeling rather than making or renewing an undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Pierre responds to Natasha’s sudden declaration of love by explaining that separation has given him a special feeling toward her. He corrects her apparent interpretation—“No, not that”—and says his love itself never ceases and cannot increase further; what is new is the post-separation quality he cannot finish articulating. Their exchanged glance completes the thought. This reflective contrast strongly supports an avowal of an enduring state rather than a promise or reflex-like discharge.
