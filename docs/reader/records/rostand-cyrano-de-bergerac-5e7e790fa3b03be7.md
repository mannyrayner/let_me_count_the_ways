## Original AI explanation

Roxane directly avows that she loves Cyrano, immediately using that avowal to support her plea that he remain alive. The love clause presents her emotional state as true. It does not independently undertake a commitment, and the urgency of the scene does not by itself establish expressive/reflexive force.

## Ambiguity

The compressed, urgent delivery is highly emotional, but there is no positive evidence that the words are a reflex-like discharge rather than an intense avowal.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Having discovered that Cyrano was the true authorial and emotional presence behind Christian’s letters, Roxane recognizes him as the single beloved she had attributed to another man. Her declaration is sincere and functions rhetorically as the premise for an urgent plea that he live. Cyrano’s fairy-tale response treats the words as potentially redemptive or transformative, but this effect does not make the core love avowal a relational undertaking or an expressive reflex.
