## Original AI explanation

The written clause primarily avows Widdowson’s wholehearted love as a fact Monica already knows. Its courtship setting gives it slight commitment-invoking force, but it does not itself clearly undertake a new obligation. The claim that affectionate words come to his lips and pen “irresistibly” provides positive, though not decisive, evidence that the language also functions as affect-driven expression rather than solely as an intense avowal.

## Ambiguity

The metapragmatic claim of irresistible expression supports E, but it may apply more directly to the surrounding endearments than to the exact declarative clause. P is also limited because romantic courtship and solicitation do not by themselves constitute an undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Widdowson uses the avowal amid anxious requests for contact and despite Monica’s instruction not to use affectionate language. It reassures and courts her while also pressing against a stated boundary. Those interpersonal pressures shape the letter’s effect but do not displace the core truth-presenting avowal. The surrounding profusion of endearments and his description of them as irresistible add some independently expressive force.
