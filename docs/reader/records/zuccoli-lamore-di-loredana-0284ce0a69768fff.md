## Original AI explanation

The complete core utterance is the negated future declaration “Non vi amerò mai” (“I shall never love you”). It primarily presents as true Loredana’s enduring absence of love for Berto. It also has a weak performative shade as a categorical rejection of his courtship, but it does not clearly promise or undertake an obligation. Nothing indicates that the words themselves are a reflex-like affective discharge rather than a forcefully delivered avowal and refusal.

## Ambiguity

The future construction and categorical “mai” can sound somewhat commissive or boundary-setting, but the dominant force is truth-conditional prediction/avowal rather than an undertaking.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Loredana interrupts Berto’s declaration of love and his hope of replacing Filippo with an unequivocal rejection. Her repeated statements that she loves only Filippo and has not come because she loves Berto strongly support a literal denial of present and future love. Her agitation and feverish physical state intensify the scene but do not independently give the utterance expressive/reflexive force.
