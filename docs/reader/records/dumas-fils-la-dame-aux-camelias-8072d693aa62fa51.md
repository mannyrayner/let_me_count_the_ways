## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

He immediately suspects that Marguerite is deceiving him, and his later observation of the Comte de G... entering her building appears to confirm at least the deception about her plans. Even if the avowal is insincere or strategically deployed, its core force remains the presentation of love as a true state rather than a new relational undertaking or expressive reflex.
