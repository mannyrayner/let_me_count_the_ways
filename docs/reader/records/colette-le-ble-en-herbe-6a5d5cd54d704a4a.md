## Original AI explanation

Vinca directly presents her love for Phil as an established fact: “tu sais bien que je t'aime mieux que tout le monde.” The utterance is also a plea for reassurance and renewed communication, but it does not itself undertake a commitment. Her distress and urgency intensify the avowal without independently making the words an expressive/reflex-like discharge.

## Ambiguity

The appeal invokes their existing bond and may have relationship-maintaining effects, but this supplies little or no performative undertaking force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Vinca says this while pleading with a jealous, withdrawn Phil not to remain angry and to speak to her. The formulation “tu sais bien” treats her love as already known and uses that avowed fact to seek reconciliation. The narrator subsequently describes her conduct in terms of the humility, awkwardness, and obstinacy of “véritable amour,” strongly framing the avowal as sincere. Her breathlessness, dismay, and hand-kissing convey intensity but do not show that the words escaped as a reflexive discharge.
