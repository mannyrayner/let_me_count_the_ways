## Original AI explanation

Lorck’s written “Jeg elsker dig virkelig Kristine” (“I really do love you, Kristine”) is principally an emphatic avowal presented as true, reinforced by his claim that he is happiest in her arms. It does not itself clearly undertake a commitment, and there is no evidence that the words are a reflex-like expressive discharge rather than a calculated written assertion.

## Ambiguity

Lorck’s sincerity is doubtful in light of his infidelity, reused love language, and self-serving reassurance, but possible deception does not reduce the utterance’s truth-conditional mode. Its relationship-maintaining effect supplies little or no independent performative force.

## Narrative situation (AI interpretation)

Lorck uses the avowal while minimizing a rival affair and reassuring the jealous Kristine that the other woman has not taken him away. The wider correspondence portrays him as serially unfaithful, conditionally affectionate, and prone to giving Kristine and Constance the same love phrases. This makes the declaration potentially manipulative or insincere, while leaving its core force that of an asserted avowal rather than a commitment or affective reflex.
