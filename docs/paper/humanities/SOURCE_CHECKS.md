## Scored passages

The 21 scored occurrences discussed are listed in [generated/case_register.json](generated/case_register.json), with immutable request/output links. Each request contains the target, local passage and supplied wider context. Quotes were compared with these requests and canonical texts; chapter context was read where needed.

First-edition page scans were visually checked for the quoted Skram passages:

- *Constance Ring*, p. 137: Lorck's declaration and offer of himself.
- *Constance Ring*, p. 309: Lorck's considered written avowal.
- *Constance Ring*, p. 370: Constance's repeated declaration after deliberate recollection.
- *Lucie*, p. 176: Henny's acceptance; subsequent context on p. 177 was read in the canonical text.

The register includes page-image links. Scans were not inserted into the repository, which already retains source maps and provenance. These local checks do not certify all OCR. The *Lucie* chapter is XVII in corrected numbering but XVI as misnumbered in the first edition.

The conditional Boarham offer is in *The Tenant of Wildfell Hall*, chapter XVI. Emma's inducement to Léon is in *Madame Bovary*, part III, chapter VII; the emotional-overflow comparison is in part II, chapter XII. Rhoda's withheld assent is in *The Odd Women*, chapter XXV. Widdowson's courtship terms occur in VII, Monica's evasion in XVI.

- [*Le Petit Prince*](https://gutenberg.net.au/ebooks03/0300771h.html), chapter IX: the rose's “Mais oui, je t'aime” and the farewell context.

- [*Up* screenplay](https://imsdb.com/scripts/Up.html), pp. 46–47: Dug's introduction and thought-translating collar. The example illustrates a hypothesis about E and was not run through the annotation pipeline.
- [*War and Peace*, Maude translation](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/2600/2600-h/2600-h.htm), book III, chapter II and book IV, chapter VI: Pierre's original formula and later judgment. The earlier self-persuasion and later repudiation are both retained.
- [Folger *King Lear*, 1.1](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/king-lear/read/1/1/): Cordelia explicitly says she loves Lear according to her bond, while refusing the demanded flattery.
- *Pengar*, [chapter 6](https://runeberg.org/pengar/06.html): Selma's recollection of repeating the wedding formula. Read against the canonical text. This is not an extracted ILY record.
- *Splash*: discussed as the proposed convergence in the repository's earlier essay, not as a newly verified or classified film transcript.

## Scope and limitations

No new membership review or classification was performed for v0.4. Film and literary illustrations outside the retained set have no invented numerical scores. The bibliography describes electronic editions, page scans and screenplays actually consulted, and keeps original publication years distinct from electronic release years. The literary readings remain open to disagreement; source verification is not independent validation of T/P/E.
